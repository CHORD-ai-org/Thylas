02 · latin + 中文 at the read step

Method — 如何衡量可比性

Two runs are comparable when the prompt set, the tokenizer, and the normalization are the same. If any of the three changes, the number changes, and we are no longer measuring the model.

当提示集、分词器与归一化三者中有任何一项发生变化时，数值就会随之变化，此时我们测量的 不再是模型本身。分母也是计量的一部分，悄悄替换分母等于替换结论。

Report Δ with a 95% interval; a point estimate alone is not a result. 置信区间与点估计 必须同时给出，否则无法判断差异是否来自随机波动。

Check: Latin baseline sits on the same line as 中文, line height holds at 26px, and no glyph falls back (watch 「，。：；「」（）」and the em dash).
