# Norse Mythology Team Naming Convention

**Date:** 2026-11-16  
**Status:** **[P]** naming convention. The candidate names below are proposals, not
team definitions. Nothing here is a confirmed preset team.

---

## 1. Naming philosophy **[P]**

Preset Coworker Teams take names from Norse mythology. A name should carry a
team's **intellectual identity, spirit, philosophy or archetype** — not just its
functional category. "Research Team" says what it does; MÍMIR says what it is
for.

The source pool is the whole mythic record, not only the Aesir: deities,
giants, other beings, realms, places, artifacts and symbols may all name a
team. The convention must cover research, engineering, science,
entrepreneurship, strategy, philosophy, cognition, creative work and whatever
categories follow — one narrow god list would not.

A name is a handle for identity. It does not grant capability, behaviour or
runtime power.

## 2. Naming-source categories **[P]**

These are places to look for names, **not** mandatory team classifications.

| Category | What it draws from | Example directions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deities | Gods and goddesses of the Norse pantheon | Sovereign intellect, strategic foresight, disruptive wit |
| Giants & mythological beings | Jötnar, elves, dwarves, serpents, other named beings | Forgecraft, boundary work, deep cognition |
| Realms & places | Nine worlds, wells, halls, forests, mountains | Interconnection, origin, crossing, isolation |
| Artifacts & symbols | Tools, weapons, rings, trees, bridges, runes | Measurement, memory, structure, passage |

A team name may come from any category regardless of what the team does.
The category is a source, never a rule.

## 3. Team identity structure **[P]**

A preset team may carry five fields. They describe identity; they do not
describe execution.

- **Team Name** — e.g. `MÍMIR`.
- **Subtitle** — e.g. `Polymath Research Collective`.
- **Mythological Archetype** — e.g. `The Keeper of Wisdom`.
- **Team Philosophy** — a short statement of worldview and purpose.
- **Team Composition** — the intended Coworker roles and responsibilities.

Only the name is required. The other fields are presentational until a team
definition is actually confirmed; none of them is an executable prompt.

## 5. Separation of naming layers **[P]**

| Layer | Names | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team Naming | ODIN, MÍMIR, YGGDRASIL | Identity of the collective |
| Coworker Naming | Mira, Protocol Reviewer, Run Archivist | Identity of one AI collaborator |
| Role Naming | Visionary, Researcher, Critic, Architect, Builder | Responsibility a member holds |
| Methodology Naming | Literature Review, Black-box Analysis, Architecture Evaluation | Method used by a Coworker or Team |

Do not force mythology-based names onto every layer. A MÍMIR team may have a
Coworker named Mira doing a Literature Review. The layers stay independent so
one can change without rewriting the others.

## 6. Boundaries **[C]**

- A Team is a collective of Coworkers, not a larger Coworker.
- A Team has its own identity and purpose; individual Coworkers keep theirs.
- Team identity does not imply a new Agent Runtime, A2A channel, scheduler or
  orchestrator. Those belong to WAM and are not implemented in Thylas.
- Naming is presentation. It grants no access, capability or execution.

## 7. Prototype note **[I]**

data using the candidate names above. It is local, read-only and has no store,
API or persistence. Real team definitions remain an open product decision.
